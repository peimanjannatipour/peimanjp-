From signals to insight

How NDMS works

The flow is intentionally legible: every stage has a clear job, and quality information travels with the signal.

01 / VIDEO

Visual dynamics

Face tracking, landmarks, movement and other visual features derived from the video stream.

02 / AUDIO

Acoustic features

Speech activity, prosody, cadence and related acoustic descriptors from synchronized audio.

03 / QUALITY

Signal quality

Video and audio quality are estimated so weak channels do not silently dominate the result.

04 / FUSION

Quality-aware fusion

Modalities are combined using context and estimated reliability rather than fixed weighting alone.

05 / TEMPORAL

State over time

Temporal dynamics matter: the system looks at trajectories, transitions and change—not only snapshots.

06 / OUTPUT

Interpretable result

Outputs include an estimate, uncertainty and comparison against a personalized baseline where available.

Why it matters

Designed around ambiguity, not certainty theater.

Human state is noisy and context dependent. The interface should surface quality, disagreement and uncertainty rather than hiding them.

↔

Cross-modal mismatch

Surfaces disagreement between channels instead of collapsing everything into one opaque score.

Q

Quality-aware inference

Weights evidence based on the quality of the underlying observation.

Δ

Change over time

Tracks trajectories and change points rather than only current-state snapshots.

H

Human-centered feedback

Outputs are meant to inform a user or researcher, not silently judge or automate consequential decisions.

Privacy-first by design

Control stays visible.

Privacy and transparency are part of the product architecture, not a footnote.

⌂

Local-first pathways

Core inference can run close to the user so raw media does not need to become the product.

✓

Consent

Data use should be explicit, granular and revocable.

+

Optional self-report

Self-report can improve research context without becoming mandatory for every use.

?

Uncertainty shown

The interface should display limits and confidence instead of presenting estimates as ground truth.

Research foundation

Multiple disciplines, one system.

The public narrative connects multimodal learning, behavioral neuroscience, timing, signal quality and human–AI interaction without turning unpublished IP into marketing copy.
